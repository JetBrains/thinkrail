The left sidebar gains a **Limits** block under the project tree: what is left on each subscription,
polled once a minute. Codex and Antigravity are read from the tokens their own tools already store,
Claude and OpenRouter through pi's credential store. A provider that refuses is not listed; OpenCode Go,
Kimi Coding and DeepSeek expose no limits and fold into one collapsed group. The block speaks
`usage.subscriptions` (`SUBSCRIPTION_USAGE_PROTOCOL_VERSION`).
