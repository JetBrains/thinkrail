# Hello ThinkRail

Welcome to the official ThinkRail blog! We're excited to share our journey building the next generation of AI-assisted development tools.

## What is ThinkRail?

ThinkRail is a desktop-and-mobile client for the `pi` coding agent. It provides a rich, modern UI that makes working with AI assistants feel natural and productive.

## Our Vision

We believe that AI-assisted development should be:

- **Intuitive** — The interface should feel like a natural extension of your workflow
- **Powerful** — Full access to the capabilities of the underlying AI
- **Fast** — No lag, no waiting, just seamless interaction

## What's Next?

In the coming weeks, we'll be sharing:

1. Deep dives into ThinkRail's architecture
2. Tips and tricks for getting the most out of `pi`
3. Behind-the-scenes looks at our development process
